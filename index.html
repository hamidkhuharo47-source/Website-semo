About the Branch

A dedicated corporate desk in the heart of Hyderabad.

Our Corporate Branch on Saddar Cantt serves employers across Hyderabad and the wider Sindh region. We specialise in Group Health Insurance — the benefit employees value most — alongside group life and accident cover, all backed by Jubilee Life Insurance Company Limited, a subsidiary of the Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development (AKFED), Switzerland.

Rather than handing you a policy document and stepping away, our team manages the whole relationship: plan design, employee enrolment, hospital panel coordination and claim settlement. You get one point of contact and a workforce that feels looked after.

  • Rated AA+ (Stable outlook) for insurer financial strength by JCR-VIS
  • Listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange; operating since June 1996
  • Shariah-compliant Window Takaful operations available for corporate plans
  • Dedicated 24/7 health emergency hotline for covered members
